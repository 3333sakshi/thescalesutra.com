THE SCALE SUTRA - WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: complete responsive one-page website.

Preview:
- Open index.html in a browser.

Publish:
1. Buy/keep your domain at your registrar.
2. Upload index.html to your hosting provider's public_html (or equivalent web root).
3. Connect the domain's DNS records as instructed by the hosting provider.
4. Enable HTTPS/SSL.
5. Test the site on mobile and desktop.

Customise before launch:
- Add your real portfolio/projects and testimonials (the starter intentionally does not invent any).
- Add your preferred social media links.
- Review service descriptions and contact details.
- Add a privacy policy if you later collect visitor information or use tracking/lead forms.

The site uses Google Fonts from an external service; it falls back to system sans-serif fonts if unavailable.
